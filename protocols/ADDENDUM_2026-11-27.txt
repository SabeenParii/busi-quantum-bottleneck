Addendum to PROTOCOL_busi_prespecified.txt (written 27 November 2026, after all main runs and analyses)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The protocol file itself is unchanged since version 1.2 (commit 2dfb883, 15 November 2026). This addendum lists
what happened after that commit, so that the record is complete. Nothing here changes the primary analysis.

A. Publication target and related work
   The protocol names a conference as the target venue. The article was later prepared for Artificial Intelligence
   in Medicine. It has not been submitted to that conference or to any other venue. The two related studies named as
   P1 and P2 are under review at the IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (P1) and will be submitted to
   Neurocomputing (P2). No image, run or result of P1 or P2 is used.

B. Deviations
   1. Initialization check. The protocol asked that the shared initial weights of all models of a seed be identical.
      The check was implemented with a tolerance for float32 rounding; the largest difference found before training
      was 2.4e-7 (column init_max_abs_diff in results/data/summaries.csv).
   2. Minimum number of epochs in 23 plan C runs. In 23 runs of plan C with seeds 3 or 4 (21 with 10% and 2 with 25%
      of the training images), a test setting left in the training notebook removed the minimum of 30 epochs, and
      training stopped after 21 to 28 epochs. The runs were retrained with the protocol settings (notebook
      06_min_epochs.ipynb); the retrained runs are part of results/data and of every reported result. The original
      runs are kept in results/original23, and Supplementary Table S12 compares the two. With the original runs no
      claim and no BUSI verdict would differ; two secondary BrEaST verdicts at 10% would be inconclusive instead of
      equivalent. The first epochs of the retrained runs repeated the original runs exactly.

C. Analyses outside the protocol (exploratory, reported as such)
   1. Loss of Dice from BUSI to each external set, compared between models (analysis/gap.py, Supplementary Table S4).
   2. Simulated hardware noise and finite shots for the 25 Q4 models of plan A (src/noise_run.py, analysis/noise.py,
      Supplementary Table S5).
   3. Retraining of Qp4 and Q8 with k = 100 (notebook 05_equal_k.ipynb, analysis/equal_k.py, Supplementary Table S11).
   4. Intervals from the five seed means alone for F1 (Supplementary Table S10).
   5. Comparison of the reported results with the original 23 runs (analysis/min_epochs.py, Supplementary Table S12).
   6. Post hoc analyses (analysis/review_extra.py): equivalence with intervals adjusted for the four F1 contrasts
      (Table S13), contrasts of each block against the identity slot (Table S14) and image-level detection with
      BUSI Dice including the normal images (Table S15). In the last, each lesion image is scored by its fold model
      and each normal image by the mean over the five fold models.
   7. The seed-level t intervals of item 4, the margin sensitivity and the 500-pixel false-positive threshold were
      all chosen after the runs.
   8. Runs added after all prespecified runs (notebook 07, analysis/rev2.py, Table S21): Tr4 and Tr8 retrained with
      k = 100, so that with the reruns of item 3 every block of F1 and F4 shares one factor, and a positive control
      Z4 whose slot outputs zeros (75 runs). Reported as exploratory.

D. Prespecified outputs
   Reported in Supplementary Tables S18 to S20 (analysis/protocol_outputs.py): pixel precision and recall
   (Section 5), the weight change of the dense layers before and after the slot (Section 6.2), the interval
   half-width of each F1 contrast as the smallest detectable difference (Section 7), and the BUSI cleaning counts
   with the difference-hash audit (Section 2.1).
   Not reported in the article: leave-one-seed-out estimates and per-seed estimates for families other than F1
   (Section 7), the gradient share at the first epoch (Section 6.3) and F1 contrasts by class (Section 7). They can be
   computed from the per-run and per-image files in results/data. Levels by class are reported in Supplementary
   Table S8. Entanglement at initialization was not recorded.
